About Mint. Mint looks for American stocks and funds that have already proven themselves, names beating their benchmark across a mix of recent windows: the past year, the past six months, and the past three months. For stocks that benchmark is VGT, a technology index; for funds it's VOO, which tracks the S&P 500. Then Mint asks the harder question: which are most likely to keep doing well over the next year, even if the market dips along the way?

The philosophy is simple: guard against big losses, but judge a pick by the year ahead, not by a rough month. We look past recent gains to the business behind each name, the risks that could derail it, and whether its success looks durable or depends on hype, headlines, or a one-time event. Strong recent performance gets a name noticed. Strong evidence earns it a spot. If nothing clears the bar, we leave the slot empty: every pick earns its place.

How it works, in three stages:

Find strength. Every stock and fund is screened for strength against its benchmark across those windows, then scored on the quality of the business, the timing of entry, and the steadiness of the ride. Hard risk gates bar names that have run too far too fast, fallen too hard, or swing too wildly.

Question the story. Survivors get researched. We read the news behind each name, not just the numbers. How much does the story depend on things outside the company's control: wars, hype, one-time windfalls? Too much, and it's out on the spot. For the rest: how likely is the strength to continue, and how much do we trust our own read?

Weigh the odds. Last, we put one honest number on each stock: its expected return, what we think it might gain over the next year, the good signs weighed against the warning signs. It is a judgment, not a promise. Its job is comparison, so every name can be ranked on the same terms and only the best odds make the cut. Funds get a grade instead of a forecast: the ETF Score, based mostly on what the fund costs, since cheap funds tend to beat expensive ones, and partly on how calmly it has behaved.
