"Est. next 1y" is a heuristic expected value, not a prediction: continuation-likelihood × (last-6-month run with a mean-reversion dampener — the first 25% counts in full, beyond that at half weight, capped at 50%, since monster half-years fade) minus break-risk × (half the historical max-drawdown magnitude) minus an event-unwind haircut (event_dependence × 20%), all scaled by the researcher's confidence in the assessment (thin/contradictory headlines → estimate shrinks toward zero).
