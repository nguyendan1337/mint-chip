How to read this. The goal is simple: the strongest names of the past year most likely to keep doing well — downside protection first. Every name here earned its place; we would rather show empty slots than filler. "1y" is the trailing one-year return (stocks vs VGT, ETFs vs VOO). "Expected value" is our honest estimate of the next year's worth — not a prediction. It balances the recent run (humbled, because hot streaks fade) against the historical pain (how deep and how often it dips), haircuts event-driven hype, rewards steady compounders over fragile spikes, and shrinks toward zero when the evidence is thin. Confidence is how much we trust the research; Stability (A = calmest) blends volatility, drawdown, and event risk. Anything below +3% expected value does not make the list. Bars use fixed scales — a full bar is +150% or +20% EV — so they stay comparable day to day. Thesis watch tracks every published pick; expand it for the list. Not financial advice.
