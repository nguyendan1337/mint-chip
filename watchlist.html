Watchlist today

The quiet names are carrying the list. Boring, contracted, cash-generating. The list's grown-ups.

Verdict: a barbell with a crack in the middle. Six Buys, ten Holds, seven Sells. The cash and contract names are healthy, the storage trade is profitable but untrustworthy at this volatility, and the story stocks are still renting their valuations from future events. The list works if the boring half funds patience with the loud half.
