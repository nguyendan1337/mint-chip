The honor roll: seven names earning their keep. VCTR (+11.1%) is strutting with a $7B First Eagle deal in its back pocket, CON (+10.2%) keeps beating like a drum with a fourth straight earnings surprise, and FTNT (+9.3%) is the tough one: disclosed a CVSS 9.8 zero-day under active exploitation Oct 1 and still earns a BUY, because the underlying business is that solid. The respectable middle: seven holds, no shame in it. VFLO (+6.7%), PK (+6.2%), and DELL (+6.2%) clear the +3% bar but can't quite convince the model they're more than decent — DELL, by the way, is behaving better this week than its broken-flag drama suggests. HNGE (+5.9%) passes but the model still won't buy it until the scarring fades, CDNA (+5.0%) clears with low confidence, and DAC (+4.7%) and DIVB (+3.7%) earn their seats with plain-faced competence. Nothing here makes your heart sing; nothing here loses you money on purpose.

The penalty box: eleven names below the bar, and some real comedy. SPCX (-16.2%) and WDC (-15.7%) are in a race to see which can disappoint harder — it's almost a sport. TSLA (-13.6%) continues its long tradition of being worth less than the vibes suggest. TTWO (-8.7%) and STX (-7.8%) are just hanging around looking expensive. NVDA (-3.5%) is the sting: the world's most important chip stock now prices in more doubt than upside, courtesy of a hot CPI and the AI-funding question nobody wants to ask twice. VGT (+0.9%), FTXL, SNDK, SPMO, QTUM round out the list of things the math says to leave alone. Eleven of twenty-five below +3%. That's not a watchlist, that's a yard sale.

The move: your list is a tale of two portfolios. Fourteen of 25 names clear the bar and the whole list averages about +1.5%, but that average is doing a lot of heavy lifting over a graveyard at the bottom. The top seven are genuinely excellent; the bottom eleven are genuinely not. If this list were a dinner party, you'd move the buys to the good table and quietly lose the sells' invitations. Trim the SELLs, hug the honor roll.
