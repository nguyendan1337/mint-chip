Watchlist today

The adults are still standing. VFLO leads the list at +4.6%, and it is not alone: Concentra (+2.8%), Acadian (+2.3%) and Victory Capital (+1.5%) all pass the pick test, and all four are buys. Behind them, DIVB (+3.7%) and CareDx (+2.1%) are the strongest holds, a dividend fund and a transplant-testing business quietly doing their jobs while the flashy names set themselves on fire. Three of the buys, VFLO, Concentra and Acadian, are also on today's Mint slate, which is the closest thing this list gets to a compliment.

The memory trade is a crime scene. Micron (-2.4%), Seagate (+0.2%), SanDisk (-0.5%) and Western Digital (-0.6%) are all sells, and they earned it: the whole complex fell 16 to 18% in a day last week and the peak-pricing question is now the whole story. Tesla (-3.8%) and SPCX (-3.4%) join them after the election fallout, and SPMO (+3.6%) is the weird one, a sell despite a positive expected value because the momentum thesis it is built on broke. Nvidia (-2.7%) only escapes the sell pile because its probe is governance, not business, but it is a hold, not a vote of confidence.

Healing, but do not call it healthy yet. The list averages +0.3% expected value, up from -5.4% on Friday, and the ratings are unchanged: 4 buys, 12 holds, 7 sells. The rebound did the lifting, not new genius. Watch tonight: Take-Two and Danaos both report after the close, and SanDisk's split record date is today.
